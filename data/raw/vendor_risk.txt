BluePeak Financial reviewed repeated service-level misses by an external document-processing vendor. The missed service levels were traced to a subcontractor queue and a manual approval gate at the vendor. Cybersecurity controls had passed the most recent assessment, and there was no evidence that a security incident caused the processing delays. The vendor's core platform remained available during the missed windows.

BluePeak required the vendor to expose subcontractor queue metrics and created an escalation threshold for items waiting beyond the agreed limit. The manual approval step was also reduced for low-risk document types. Service-level performance improved after the changes, though BluePeak kept the vendor on enhanced monitoring for another quarter.
