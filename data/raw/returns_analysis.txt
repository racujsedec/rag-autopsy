Ember Apparel reviewed a rise in online product returns across several clothing categories. The review found that inconsistent garment sizing and product imagery that understated fit differences were the two strongest drivers of returns. Shipping damage accounted for only a small share of returned items, and delivery speed was similar for returned and retained orders.

Merchandising added more detailed fit notes, standardized size measurements across suppliers, and commissioned new photography for products with the highest return rates. Return rates improved for the first updated categories. The company also began tracking return reasons by supplier so sizing inconsistency can be detected earlier in the assortment cycle.
