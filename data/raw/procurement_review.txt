Meridian Appliances reviewed supplier performance after component lead times increased during the summer. Late component arrivals were driven mainly by a concentration of orders at two suppliers and longer customs clearance at one port. Spot freight was used on several urgent shipments, but transportation capacity itself was generally available. The review did not find a drop in customer demand to be the primary reason lead times worsened.

Procurement responded by shifting part of the order volume to an approved secondary supplier and by submitting customs documentation earlier for high-volume components. The team also introduced a weekly exception review for purchase orders approaching their promised ship dates. Lead-time variability improved over the following month, although a small number of specialty components remained exposed to the original suppliers.
