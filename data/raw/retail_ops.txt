Northstar Retail operates a regional distribution network serving stores and direct-to-consumer orders. During the spring quarter, fulfillment delays increased primarily because inbound supplier shipments arrived later than planned and several high-volume items required manual inventory reconciliation. The operations team responded by increasing safety-stock thresholds for selected products and by adding an exception queue for inventory mismatches. Delivery performance improved after the new controls were introduced, although expedited shipping costs remained elevated for several weeks.

The analytics team tracks order cycle time, fill rate, cancellation rate, inventory accuracy, and expedited shipping cost. A later review found that inaccurate inventory counts were more strongly associated with cancellations than warehouse labor availability. This finding changed the team's prioritization of remediation work.
