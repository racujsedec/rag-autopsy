Arcadia Components reported stable revenue but lower operating margin during the second quarter. The margin decline was driven mainly by higher logistics expense, temporary supplier surcharges, and an unfavorable product mix. Management noted that demand remained healthy and that unit volume increased in the enterprise segment.

The finance team expects some logistics costs to normalize over the next two quarters. However, supplier contract renewals may keep input costs above the prior-year level. The company therefore plans to focus on pricing discipline, procurement negotiations, and a gradual reduction in expedited freight. Management did not attribute the margin decline to lower customer demand.
