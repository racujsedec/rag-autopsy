Beacon Distribution experienced intermittent delays at handheld scanning stations during a high-volume receiving shift. Investigation found that a recent scanner firmware release was repeatedly retrying failed batch submissions, creating a queue of duplicate requests. Wireless signal strength remained within the expected range, and the network team did not find evidence that warehouse Wi-Fi coverage caused the slowdown.

The team rolled back the firmware and reduced the batch retry setting, after which scan latency returned to normal. A staged firmware rollout was added so that future releases first reach a small group of devices. Operations also created an alert for unusually high retry counts because the original incident was visible in request volume before employees reported slow scans.
