A data platform incident caused several scheduled analytics jobs to miss their service-level targets. The initial symptom was a sharp increase in query latency. Investigation showed that a recent schema change increased shuffle volume and produced severe data skew in one of the largest joins. The cluster itself remained healthy.

Engineers mitigated the incident by changing the partitioning strategy and introducing a broadcast join for a small reference dataset. They also added a regression test that compares shuffle volume and stage duration against historical baselines. No data was lost during the incident.
