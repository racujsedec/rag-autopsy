Silverline Software investigated a burst of suspicious login activity against its customer portal. The attack consisted mostly of automated credential-stuffing attempts against the customer portal. Multi-factor authentication prevented privileged administrator accounts from being compromised, but many customer accounts did not yet require MFA. The security team found no evidence that the attackers exploited a software vulnerability in the portal.

Silverline added more aggressive IP throttling, expanded breached-password screening, and accelerated MFA enrollment for customer accounts. The team also introduced a detection rule for rapid login attempts distributed across many usernames. Login-abuse volume declined after the controls were enabled, although credential-stuffing attempts continued at a lower rate.
