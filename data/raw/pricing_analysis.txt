Summit Office Products reviewed why operating margin improved even though unit volume remained nearly flat. The improvement came mainly from selective list-price increases and tighter controls on discount leakage. Lower expedited-freight expense helped slightly, but it was not the primary contributor. Product mix was broadly similar to the previous quarter.

Sales operations added approval thresholds for unusually large discounts and created a weekly report comparing realized price with list price by account. The company kept the new controls after margins remained stronger for a second reporting period. Management cautioned that further broad price increases could affect demand in more price-sensitive customer segments.
