Lumen Telecom saw its customer-support backlog rise after a product launch and several account-security changes. The largest contributor to the backlog was a new identity-verification step that increased average handling time, especially for account-recovery cases. Call volume also increased, but staffing levels remained close to plan. The review did not identify agent headcount as the primary cause of the backlog.

Support simplified the verification workflow for low-risk cases and created a callback queue for customers who could not complete identity checks during the first contact. Supervisors also began tracking verification time separately from total handling time. The backlog declined over the next two weeks even though incoming contact volume remained above its earlier average.
